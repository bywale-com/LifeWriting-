## I. The brief that opened this file

The founder’s working model, recorded in their wording:

> Relationships have a disposition/state toward a person, separate from momentary emotion. Someone can be deeply positively disposed toward you while angry, or negatively disposed while calm.

> I model disposition roughly on a spectrum: Deep Blue → Blue → Blue-Gray → Gray → Gray-Red → Red → Deep Red.

> There seem to be at least two major paths of movement:
>
> Relative path: familiarity, repeated interaction, shared experience, continuity, accumulated ease.
>
> Absolute/evidence path: high-weight evidence that updates a person’s model of you—honesty, loyalty, competence, reliability, costly signals, betrayal, etc.

> Rule of thumb: relative path is especially useful socially; absolute/evidence path is especially useful structurally, though both operate everywhere.

The terminal goal was stated as deeper than “build trust” or “be charismatic”:

> Suppose I want people around me to be able to be: playful, bold, relaxed, daring, open, candid, enthusiastic, curious, happy, expressive, willing to disagree, and generally alive.

> Continually create situations in which desirable forms of expression are possible and safe, while reducing unnecessary conditions that make people shrink, guard themselves, or self-monitor.

> Or: Create the conditions under which the other person can express their best relational state with you.

Explicit exclusions: manipulation, forced positivity, people-pleasing, eliminating disagreement, making everyone comfortable. Challenge, tension, and boundaries remain in scope.

A tentative routine was offered and **not** to be treated as correct:

> Read state → identify inhibitor/enabler → create enabling condition → invite expression → reinforce/update

The instruction to the researcher:

> Do not force everything into my existing framework. I specifically want findings that challenge, subdivide, replace, or reveal missing pieces in the model.

## II. What the literatures will not let the model keep

These are the load-bearing corrections. Vocabulary mapping comes after.

### 1. One spectrum cannot hold the state

The architecture already separates color from current emotion. That is right, and Gottman is the cleanest empirical ally: anger is not a Horseman; contempt is. **[Evidence]** Gottman, Coan, Carrère, and Swanson (1998), *Journal of Marriage and Family*. https://psycnet.apa.org/record/1998-00570-001

What the spectrum still collapses:

**Trust and distrust can coexist.** Trust = confident positive expectations; distrust = confident negative expectations. High/high is a mixed working relationship (rely on competence, watch politics). Low/low is indifference, not Gray-as-midpoint. **[Evidence]** Lewicki, McAllister, and Bies (1998), *Academy of Management Review* 23(3). https://doi.org/10.5465/amr.1998.926620

**Warmth and competence split independently.** High competence / low warmth is admired-and-guarded, not simply Red. High warmth / low competence is liked-and-not-relied-upon, not simply Blue-Gray. **[Evidence]** Fiske, Cuddy, Glick, and Xu (2002), Stereotype Content Model, *JPSP* 82(6).

**Affiliation and dominance are separate axes.** Momentary “Red” in a meeting is often a control move, not a latent state. Friendliness begets friendliness; hostility begets hostility; dominance tends to beget submission — except hostile-dominant, which often elicits counter-hostility. **[Evidence]** Kiesler (1983), *Psychological Review* 90(3); Orford (1986) qualification.

**Attachment is two-dimensional.** Anxiety × avoidance, not secure↔insecure. High investment plus low reachability is closer to anxiety than to “mid-Red.” **[Evidence]** Bartholomew and Horowitz (1991); Feeney and Thrush (2010) below.

**Trustworthiness is at least three facets.** Ability, benevolence, integrity, plus the trustor’s propensity. They do not move as one. **[Evidence]** Mayer, Davis, and Schoorman (1995), *AMR* 20(3); Colquitt, Scott, and LePine (2007) meta, *JAP* 92(4).

**[Inference]** Packing investment, trust, reachability, slack, and adversarial potential onto Deep Blue → Deep Red will confuse at least four mixed cells: anxious high-investment/low-reachability; avoidant low-investment/low-surface-hostility; high-trust/high-conflict; high-warmth/high-control. Color remains useful as a *summary of slack and presumed continuity*. It is a poor state vector.

### 2. Trust is a means. Exploration is a different outcome.

The stated goal is not a warmer color. It is play, boldness, candor, curiosity, willingness to disagree. Attachment theory already named that outcome: **exploration**, enabled by a **secure base**.

**[Evidence]** Feeney and Thrush (2010), *JPSP* 98(1), 167 couples: three independent characteristics of a secure base — **availability**, **noninterference**, **encouragement** — each predict exploration behavior. Anxious partners interfere or under-encourage; avoidant partners are unavailable. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0016961 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20053031/

**[Evidence]** Feeney (2007), *JPSP*: the **dependency paradox** — accepting dependence *when needed* predicts more autonomy and independent goal-striving over six months, not clinginess.

**[Inference]** Blueward movement and “people come alive around you” can diverge. A controlling Blue (high warmth, high interference, contingent regard) produces relatedness without exploration. That is the opposite of the stated goal, and the spectrum has no cell for it.

### 3. Relative vs evidence is too few paths

The two-path split is directionally right and empirically underspecified.

Mechanisms that are **not** the relative path and **not** the evidence path:

- **Mere exposure** increases liking without interaction or character data. **[Evidence]** Zajonc (1968); Bornstein (1989) meta.
- **Propinquity** produces contact, not trustworthiness. **[Evidence]** Festinger, Schachter, and Back (1950).
- **Ambient belonging** is inferred from objects and layout before any ABI evidence. **[Evidence]** Cheryan, Plaut, Davies, and Steele (2009), *JPSP*.
- **Swift / role trust** is imported from categories and tasks, then verified. **[Evidence]** Meyerson, Weick, and Kramer (1996).
- **Calculus-based trust** can exist with no warmth (deterrence, monitoring, contracts). **[Evidence]** Lewicki and Bunker (1996).
- **More information can reduce liking** via dissimilarity cascades. People *believe* the opposite. **[Evidence]** Norton, Frost, and Ariely (2007), *JPSP*.

What actually grows *faith* rather than mere predictability is **diagnostic situations**: mixed motives, high interdependence, partner forgoes self-interest. **[Evidence]** Holmes and Rempel (1989); Simpson (2007), *Current Directions in Psychological Science* 16(5). https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8721.2007.00517.x

**[Inference]** Hours together are a weak prior. They can accumulate low-stakes responsiveness (bids, capitalization), or they can accumulate contempt. The relative path only works when it is secretly a stream of low-LR evidence of *perceived responsiveness*, not when it is time.

The social-vs-structural rule of thumb also over-assigns. Diagnostic integrity tests matter socially (betrayal in friendship). Familiarity and role-trust matter structurally (you can work with someone for years on calculus-based trust). Both paths operate everywhere; which one *moves the load-bearing variable* depends on the relationship type, not on a social/structural partition.

### 4. The tentative routine over-invites

“Invite expression” is sometimes the inhibitor.

Secure-base **noninterference** and SDT **autonomy support** say: often the enabling condition is not asking, not taking over, and not making expression a relational assignment. Capitalization and Gottman bids are invitations **the other already made**. Pulling disclosure from a self-protecting risk-regulation state can confirm the threat.

**[Evidence]** Murray, Holmes, and Collins (2006), risk regulation, *Psychological Bulletin* 132(5): when perceived regard is low, hurtful moments trigger self-protection (withdraw, preemptive hostility), not more candor. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.5.641

**[Evidence]** Patrick, Knee, Canevello, and Lonsbary (2007); Knee et al. (2005): warmth that is controlling is not need-support. People feel most related to those who support their *autonomy*. Forcing a trade of autonomy for relatedness degrades both.

**[Inference]** Replace the engine of the routine. Read state and identify inhibitors, yes. Then the default enabling move is often **availability without demand**. Invitation is optional and downstream.

### 5. The update is perceived, not enacted

**[Evidence]** Reis, Clark, and Holmes (2004): **perceived partner responsiveness** (PPR) is the belief that the partner understands, validates, and cares about central features of the self. Intimacy is the perception, not the deed. Enacted support often *hurts* unless it is perceived as responsive (Maisel and Gable, 2009).

**[Inference]** A well-designed “enabling condition” that is not seen as understanding / validation / care does not move the working model. Asymmetry-seeking that maps only *your* intent will mis-score movement.

### 6. “Relational entropy” is not a construct. Dormancy contradicts simple decay.

Relationship science uses **maintenance**, **tie decay**, **dormancy**, **boredom / self-expansion stall**. Entropy is a metaphor.

**[Evidence]** Stafford and Canary (1991): positivity, assurances, and sharing tasks predict satisfaction and commitment; openness is weaker than folk theory expects.

**[Evidence]** Burt (2002), “Bridge Decay”: nine in ten network bridges are gone the next year.

**[Evidence]** Levin, Walter, and Murnighan (2011): **dormant ties** unused ≥3 years, when reconnected, often yield more novel useful knowledge than current ties; trust and shared perspective persist in memory. Unused ≠ Deep Red. Unused ≠ dead.

**[Evidence]** Aron, Norman, Aron, McKenna, and Heyman (2000): shared *novel* activity restores satisfaction when self-expansion stalls. Habituation is not hostility.

## III. Established names for the phenomenon (Q1, Q11)

There is no single field called “creating interpersonal conditions for aliveness.” There is a cluster. The closest names, with what they actually cover:

| Name | Discipline | What it actually enables | What it does not |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Secure base / safe haven** (Bowlby; Feeney) | Attachment | Exploration, play, independent striving | Team voice; liking |
| **Perceived partner responsiveness** (Reis) | Relationship science | Disclosure, intimacy, further bidding | Structural safety under a boss |
| **Psychological safety** (Edmondson 1999) | Organizational behavior | Interpersonal risk-taking: ask, admit, dissent in a *team* | Play, warmth, dyadic attachment |
| **Felt security** | Adult attachment | Confidence this specific other is a base | Climate among several others |
| **Autonomy support** (Deci & Ryan; Knee) | SDT | Volitional expression, less defensiveness in conflict | Competence or relatedness by itself |
| **Michelangelo phenomenon** (Rusbult, Finkel, Kumashiro) | Interdependence | Movement toward the other’s *ideal self* | General trust |
| **High-quality connections** (Dutton & Heaphy) | Positive organizational scholarship | Vitality, regard, tensile strength in *brief* interactions | A long relationship |
| **Working alliance** (Bordin) | Psychotherapy | Bond + agreed goals + agreed tasks | Friendship; no role contract |
| **Epistemic trust** (Fonagy) | Developmental psychopathology | Treating the other’s communication as personally relevant knowledge | Play; warmth |
| **Rapport** (Tickle-Degnen & Rosenthal 1990) | Social psychology | Mutual attentiveness, positivity, coordination | Trustworthiness; slack |
| **Voice climate** (Morrison) | OB | Whether speaking up is worth it | Exploration of a personal self |

**[Inference]** The founder’s goal is a *conjunction* of several of these, not a synonym of any one. Play and candor and disagreement have different inhibitors. Psychological safety is the closest *organizational* name and the most over-applied popular one. It licenses learning behavior, not play. Secure-base provision is the closest *dyadic* name for “come alive / explore.” PPR is the closest *process* name for how a person concludes “this other is for me.”

**Terminology that can replace or subdivide the house vocabulary:**

- **Relational disposition** → keep as slack + presumed continuity (the Deep Blue “we are still us”). Add a state vector beside it: PPR / felt regard, secure-base usability, autonomy-support vs control, epistemic openness, cognitive trust, affective trust, distrust, momentary affect.
- **Relative path** → split into mere exposure, propinquity, knowledge-based predictability, micro-responsiveness (bids/ACR), self-expansion novelty.
- **Absolute / evidence path** → **trust-diagnostic situations** (Simpson) + **costly signals** + **violation-type diagnosticity** (Kim: integrity vs competence).
- **Movement condition** → keep. Add **diagnosticity** (LR of the event) and **perceived** (not enacted) responsiveness.
- **Social temperature** → local rapport / high-quality connection / immediate complementarity. Keep distinct from climate.
- **Structural temperature** → LMX + formal authority + voice climate + propagation influence. This house distinction survives and is one of the stronger original pieces.
- **Relational entropy** → drop as a term. Use **maintenance**, **tie decay**, **dormancy**, **self-expansion stall**.
- **Trust model** → person-specific **internal working model** (Collins & Read hierarchy: global → class → this person) weighted by **ABI**, **rejection sensitivity**, and **epistemic trust**.
- **Enabling conditions** → the secure-base triad + autonomy support + decline-without-cost + (if you have rank) leader inclusiveness and fallibility modeling.

## IV. Inhibitors and enablers (Q2, Q3)

These are not one “anxiety” pile. They have different signatures and different enabling moves.

### Inhibitors (constriction)

**Social-evaluative threat.** Performance that can be negatively judged by others. Combined with uncontrollability it produces the largest cortisol responses. Not all stress does this. **[Evidence]** Dickerson and Kemeny (2004) meta, 208 laboratory studies, *Psychological Bulletin* 130(3). Combined social-evaluative + uncontrollable tasks, *d* ≈ .93; without those elements, near zero. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.130.3.355

**Evaluation apprehension / objective self-awareness.** An audience turns attention inward; self-monitoring rises; spontaneity falls. Distinct from distrust of a specific person.

**Status threat.** Rank, competence display, or public correction that can move standing. Especially potent under hierarchy. Distinct from liking.

**Distrust (confident negative expectation).** Prediction of harm, exploitation, or bad faith. Separate from low trust (absence of positive expectation). **[Evidence]** Lewicki et al. (1998).

**Shame / humiliation risk.** Self as globally diminished in the eyes of others. Different from guilt (act-focused). Punishes candor and play; produces hiding, not repair.

**Anticipated punishment of voice.** The Edmondson item: “If you make a mistake on this team, it is often held against you.” This can exist in a warm room.

**Uncertainty / unpredictability.** Cannot forecast responses; learned vigilance. Distinct from known harshness (which is at least modellable).

**Lack of autonomy / controlling relatedness.** Pressure, guilt, contingent warmth. Produces reactance or compliance-without-aliveness. **[Evidence]** Brehm (1966) reactance; Ryan and Deci (2000); Vansteenkiste and Ryan (2013) need thwarting.

**Interference.** Taking over the other’s task or expression. Directly suppresses exploration even when the interferer is available and encouraging. **[Evidence]** Feeney and Thrush (2010).

**Power-down inhibition.** Reduced power → threat vigilance, constrained behavior. **[Evidence]** Keltner, Gruenfeld, and Anderson (2003), *Psychological Review* 110(2).

**Self-protection under low perceived regard.** Risk regulation: expression is a dependence risk. **[Evidence]** Murray, Holmes, and Collins (2006).

### Enablers (expansion)

These are observable. They are not “be charismatic.”

**Availability without takeover.** Findable if needed; does not occupy the activity. Secure-base availability + noninterference.

**Encouragement of the other’s excursion.** Explicit support for *their* attempt, not a joint performance.

**Understanding, validation, caring — as perceived.** PPR. Validation ≠ agreement. **[Evidence]** Reis and Shaver (1988) intimacy process; Linehan (1997): validate the valid, refuse to validate the invalid.

**Active-constructive response to good news.** Questions that relive the event, matched energy, naming why it matters to *them*. The other three response types (passive-constructive, active-destructive, passive-destructive) are negatively associated with intimacy. **[Evidence]** Gable, Reis, Impett, and Asher (2004), *JPSP*; Gable, Gonzaga, and Strachman (2006).

**Turning toward bids.** Small reaches (comment, joke, sigh, ask) answered at text and subtext. Gottman lab: stayers turned toward ~86% of bids, divorcers ~33%. **[Evidence]** Gottman observational program; treat as couples data, not a universal constant.

**Autonomy support in conflict.** Take the other’s frame; no guilt; keep relatedness and disagreement in the same conversation. **[Evidence]** Knee et al. (2005); Patrick et al. (2007).

**Decline without cost.** The other can say no, dissent, or not disclose without losing standing, access, or warmth. This is the non-manipulation test and an enabling condition for diagnostic evidence.

**Leader inclusiveness and fallibility modeling** (if you hold rank). Invite and appreciate contributions, especially from lower status; acknowledge your own miss; thank the first correction. **[Evidence]** Nembhard and Edmondson (2006), 23 NICUs, 1,440 respondents: inclusiveness moderates the status → safety gap. https://doi.org/10.1002/job.413

**Load-sharing presence.** Social Baseline Theory: the brain’s default ecology is proximity to trusted others; presence conserves resources. Quality, not mere spouse-status, drives neural attenuation of threat. **[Evidence]** Beckes and Coan (2011); Coan, Schaefer, and Davidson (2006), *Psychological Science*.

**Play frame.** Bateson: a signal that “this is not the serious frame” (the play bow). Play is inhibited by fear, hunger, and unresolved threat (ethology). Improv “yes-and” locally lowers idea-evaluation (Limb and Braun: improvisation associated with reduced dorsolateral prefrontal self-monitoring in musicians). **[Inference]** A play frame enables play and idea generation. It does **not** automatically enable candor about disagreement. “Yes-and” can suppress a valid no. Do not use improv rules as a candor protocol.

**Rapport as coordination over time.** Early: positivity + attentiveness. Later: coordination + attentiveness; positivity can drop without loss of rapport. **[Evidence]** Tickle-Degnen and Rosenthal (1990), *Psychological Inquiry* 1(4). **[Inference]** Forced positivity in a mature Blue relationship is a misapplied early-rapport move. Matches the architecture’s “Deep Blue + furious.”

**Chameleon / mimicry** increases smoothness and liking when unintentional. Deliberate mimicry is often detected and then costs. **[Evidence]** Chartrand and Bargh (1999). Not a practice target.

## V. Movement, maintenance, power (Q4, Q5, Q8)

### What actually changes disposition

**Low-diagnostic accumulation (the repaired relative path).** Repeated unpunished bids, ACR, availability, shared tasks, successful micro-conflict. Builds **knowledge-based trust** (predictability) and, if perceived as responsive, PPR. Can stall at calculus- or knowledge-based trust and never reach identification. **[Evidence]** Lewicki and Bunker (1996).

**High-diagnostic events (the repaired evidence path).** A partner, in a conflict of interest, chooses the relationship or the other’s welfare at a cost. Public defense, hidden-error admission, keeping a confidence when gossip would pay. Integrity-relevant negatives are more diagnostic than positives; ability-relevant positives are more diagnostic than negatives. **[Evidence]** Reeder–Brewer / Skowronski–Carlston schematic attribution, applied to trust repair by Kim, Ferrin, Cooper, and Dirks (2004), *JAP* 89(1): apology repairs **competence** violations better than denial; denial (when claiming innocence) repairs alleged **integrity** violations better than apology. One integrity miss outweighs many later goods. https://ink.library.smu.edu.sg/lkcsb_research/2368/

**Rupture–repair.** Deterioration in bond, goals, or tasks, then naming and owning. Alliance–outcome *r* ≈ .28 (Flückiger, Del Re, Wampold, and Horvath, 2018, 295 studies). Rupture-*repair* ↔ outcome *r* ≈ .29. Rupture-resolution *training* effect on outcome is weak overall. **[Evidence]** Eubanks, Muran, and Safran (2018). **[Inference]** Repair is a high-weight update. Training people to “do repair” as a script is not the same as repair happening.

**Michelangelo affirmation.** Seeing and eliciting the other’s ideal-self behaviors. Directional, not a general trust meter. Can become controlling if the “ideal” is the sculptor’s agenda. **[Evidence]** Drigotas, Rusbult, Wieselquist, and Whitton (1999), *JPSP*; Rusbult, Finkel, and Kumashiro (2009) review.

**Punishment of voice / public humiliation.** Moves climate and, if the punisher has rank, structural temperature. Can leave dyadic liking intact.

**[Inference]** The architecture’s “surface cue → person-specific interpretation → latent trust/distrust update → spectrum movement” survives. It needs **which facet** is updating (A, B, I, distrust, epistemic gate, secure-base usability) and **whether the recipient perceived it**.

### What maintains a state

Active ties: positivity, assurances of continuation, sharing tasks, occasional novel joint work. Openness/disclosure as a *maintenance strategy* is weaker than folk theory. **[Evidence]** Stafford and Canary (1991).

Silence is not automatically decay. Dormant ties can retain trust in memory. **[Evidence]** Levin et al. (2011).

Habituation / boredom is a self-expansion stall, not Redward movement. The move is shared novelty, not more reassurance.

Identification-based trust, once formed, is stickier than calculus-based trust and more devastated by integrity violation.

### How power changes all of it

Power increases approach, decreases inhibition, and **reduces spontaneous perspective-taking**. **[Evidence]** Keltner et al. (2003); Galinsky, Magee, Inesi, and Gruenfeld (2006), *Psychological Science* 17(12).

Enabling **from above** changes the cost function of the other’s expression. Peer warmth cannot cancel a punitive boss. Nembhard and Edmondson: status predicts safety unless the leader is inclusive.

**[Inference]** This is the mechanism under the house’s social vs structural temperature. A Blue coworker set plus a Red boss is not average Gray. Structural temperature is LMX + formal authority + who transmits interpretations.

If you hold rank, enabling is a **routine you run against your own reduced perspective-taking**, not a feeling of being approachable.

If you do not hold rank, expect the MUM effect (withholding bad news) and treat your own inhibition as context, not as your color toward the person. **[Evidence]** Rosen and Tesser (1970).

Boss/subordinate also changes **legitimate ceiling** of interior dimensions — already in the alarm note (relationship type). Disclosure, play, and candor have different legal shapes under hierarchy. Psychological safety is the licensed form of candor at work; secure-base play often is not.

## VI. Manipulation vs genuine enabling (Q7)

The literature’s test is not inner purity of motive. It is **structure**.

| Enabling | Manipulation / control |
|---|---|
| Other can decline without cost | No is expensive (status, access, warmth) |
| Autonomy support: frame, rationale, choice | Pressure, guilt, contingent regard |
| Validation of the valid | Agreement demanded, or invalid treated as valid |
| Secure-base noninterference | “Invite expression” as assignment |
| Diagnostic situation that occurs, or a cheap ask | Manufactured loyalty test |
| Influence attempt that can be recognized and refused | Covert persuasion once detected (Friestad & Wright, 1994) produces coping, not trust |
| Michelangelo toward *their* stated ideal | Pygmalion toward *your* preferred self |

**[Evidence]** Psychological reactance (Brehm, 1966): threatened freedom → restore it, including doing the opposite. Controlling Blueward effort can produce iatrogenic Redward movement that looks like “they were never going to trust.”

**[Inference]** Discovery’s Bait/Probe stays inside enabling only if decline is free. The previous Discovery dossier already said this; SDT and reactance are the mechanism.

## VII. Individual differences (Q10) and what is trainable (Q9)

### What predicts different “trust models”

**Internal working models are hierarchical and person-specific.** Global → relationship-class → *this person*. Large within-person variation in security across figures, predicted by need fulfillment with *that* person. **[Evidence]** Collins and Read (1994); La Guardia, Ryan, Couchman, and Deci (2000), *JPSP*; Overall, Fletcher, and Friesen (2003).

**ABI weights differ.** One person updates most on integrity, another on ability, another on benevolence. Propensity to trust is a prior before information. **[Evidence]** Mayer et al. (1995); Colquitt et al. (2007).

**Rejection sensitivity.** Anxious expectation of rejection → hypervigilance for rejection cues → over-perception of intentional rejection in ambiguous behavior → overreaction that can produce the feared outcome. Not redundant with attachment style, neuroticism, or self-esteem. **[Evidence]** Downey and Feldman (1996), *JPSP*. https://psychology.columbia.edu/sites/psychology.columbia.edu/files/2016-11/merp.pdf

**Epistemic trust vs vigilance.** Whose communication is treated as personally relevant knowledge. Can be closed after adversity even when warmth is present. Ostensive cues (being mentalized as a subject) open it. **[Evidence]** Fonagy and Allison (2014).

**Attachment orientation filters diagnostic data.** Same costly signal, different update. **[Evidence]** Simpson (2007).

**Entity vs incremental lay theories** (Dweck) affect whether a single miss is treated as a type or as a state. Not relationship-specific, but it changes the evidence path.

**[Inference]** The house “personal trust evidence model” is already the right *idea*. The research names how to *read* it: watch what they treat as load-bearing (integrity vs competence vs autonomy vs loyalty), how they read ambiguity (RS), whether they take your words as about them (epistemic trust), and whether this figure is an exception to their global model (La Guardia within-person variation). Behavior reveals it, as the alarm note said. There is no validated “interview for someone’s trust model.” There are diagnostic situations and pattern accumulation — Discovery.

### What is trainable (behaviors, not traits)

Trainable with feedback, not as slogans:

- Secure-base triad: be findable, do not take over, encourage *their* attempt.
- PPR response to a self-relevant disclosure: show the core was understood; respect the viewpoint; care about welfare; do not convert to advice unless asked.
- Active-constructive capitalization.
- Turn toward bids; do not require cleverness.
- Validation without agreement (Linehan levels, stopping at what is actually valid).
- Autonomy support in conflict: their frame first; no contingent warmth.
- If you have rank: humble inquiry (Schein: questions to which you do not know the answer); named invitation; fallibility modeling; thank the correction.
- Score A, B, I, and distrust separately; score climate separately from dyad.
- Repair typed by violation: competence → apology + changed behavior; integrity → do not treat niceness as repair.
- Decline-without-cost on asks.
- After-action on missed bids / over-invitations (the Discovery dossier’s AAR finding applies).

Not trainable as a general skill, or actively counterproductive as a target:

- Charisma as a trait. Antonakis treats it as signaling; it can raise status threat.
- Deliberate mimicry.
- Forced 5:1 positivity. Gottman 1998 found anger is not the danger; manufactured smile ratios are the architecture’s forbidden forced positivity.
- One-day psychological-safety workshops without leader behavior change.
- “Be more trustworthy” without domain-specific ABI evidence.
- Improv as a candor protocol.

## VIII. Synthesis

### A. Conceptual map — closest established bodies of knowledge

1. **Attachment / felt security / secure-base provision** — closest to the *goal* (exploration, play, boldness) and to person-specific working models.
2. **Perceived partner responsiveness** — closest to the *process* by which a person concludes the other is for them.
3. **Risk regulation** — closest to *why* someone who “should” trust still will not be candid.
4. **Self-determination theory (autonomy support vs control)** — closest to the anti-manipulation clause and to the controlling-Blue failure mode.
5. **Psychological safety + voice climate + LMX** — closest to *structural* enabling, especially under authority. Not a synonym of Blue.
6. **Mayer ABI + Lewicki trust/distrust + McAllister cognitive/affective** — closest to subdividing “trust.”
7. **Simpson diagnostic situations + Kim repair asymmetry** — closest to the evidence path, specified.
8. **Gottman bids / Horsemen** and **Gable capitalization** — closest specified *micro-routines*, with a couples-data limit.
9. **Michelangelo phenomenon** — closest to “best relational state” as ideal-self expression, not as trust.
10. **Epistemic trust** — closest to “whose signals I treat as about me,” missing from the spectrum.
11. **Stafford maintenance, Burt decay, Levin dormancy, Aron self-expansion** — closest to maintenance/decay, replacing entropy.
12. **Interpersonal circumplex + SCM warmth/competence** — closest 2D replacements for collapsed color in the moment and in mixed evaluations.

Discovery remains the information engine for reading inhibitors, enablers, and person-specific weights. This file is the *use* of that information to create conditions — or to refrain.

### B. Mechanism map — expansion vs constriction

**Constriction.** Social-evaluative threat + uncontrollability (cortisol). Status threat under hierarchy. Confident distrust. Shame. Punishment of voice. Unpredictability. Controlling relatedness. Interference. Power-down inhibition. Low perceived regard → self-protection. Evaluation that turns attention inward.

**Expansion.** Availability + noninterference + encouragement. Perceived understanding/validation/caring. Unpunished voice. Decline without cost. Load-sharing presence of a *quality* other. Play frame (for play, not for dissent). Autonomy support during disagreement. Active-constructive response to bids the other already made.

**The mediating belief** is usually some form of: “I can take a risk in your presence and remain intact — in standing, in the bond, in autonomy, or in the epistemic channel.” Which of those four is live depends on the person and the relationship type.

**[Inference]** “Remove unnecessary social threat while preserving truth, autonomy, play, candor, and mutual freedom” is a good principle. The mechanism is not removing all threat. Diagnostic situations *are* threatening. The move is removing *non-diagnostic* threat (evaluation theater, interference, contingent warmth, costly nos) so that remaining threat is informative rather than shrinking.

### C. Movement-condition taxonomy

| Class | Examples | Typical facet moved | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro-responsiveness | Turn toward bid; ACR; PPR reflection | PPR, affective trust, felt regard | Relative path *when it works* |
| Predictability accumulation | Repeated cooperation, shared tasks | Cognitive / knowledge-based trust | Can stall; not exploration |
| Exposure / propinquity | Mere contact, seating, décor-fit | Liking, ambient belonging | Easy false Blue-Gray |
| Diagnostic / costly | Conflict of interest, public defense, hidden-error admission | Integrity or benevolence | High LR; do not manufacture |
| Competence display | Delivery under pressure | Ability | Easier to repair than integrity |
| Voice incident | Error unpunished or punished, especially by rank | Climate, structural temperature | Not dyadic color |
| Rupture–repair | Named strain, owned contribution | Bond, epistemic trust | High weight if perceived |
| Integrity violation | Betrayal, humiliation, using disclosure | Distrust, IWM | Niceness does not repair |
| Interference / control | Takeover, contingent regard, forced invitation | Autonomy, exploration | Can happen inside Blue |
| Novelty / self-expansion | Shared new activity | Satisfaction, vitality | Counters stall, not Red |
| Maintenance failure | Stopped assurances/tasks on an *active* tie | Predictability | Not the same as dormancy |
| Dormancy | Unused, previously clean tie | None, or memory-trust | Reconnection ≠ repair from Red |
| Power move | Hostile-dominant challenge | Complementarity this turn | Often not a state change |

Load-bearing conditions in the alarm note (loyalty, honesty, autonomy, confidentiality, respect, reciprocity, reliability) map onto ABI + autonomy. Keep them. Add **voice-punishment** and **interference** as load-bearing for the *aliveness* goal specifically.

### D. Routine library

The tentative loop is replaced. Discovery still runs. Enabling does not always invite.

**Recurring processes a person can actually execute** in a new workplace, friendship, team, or authority relationship:

1. **Map referents, not the room.** For anyone who can affect you, keep separate columns: Ability, Benevolence, Integrity, Distrust, Climate (what happens when a low-status person errs), LMX/structural reach. Do not average into a color.

2. **Watch unpunished and punished voice.** First meetings: who asks, who admits, what the highest-status person does next. That is climate data.

3. **Tag ease correctly.** Proximity, repeated exposure, and décor-fit raise ease. Label them exposure/ambient belonging, not ABI.

4. **Secure-base default.** Be available. Do not take over. Encourage *their* attempt. Do not demand a performance of openness.

5. **Respond to bids they already made.** Capitalization and small reaches. Active-constructive for good news. Turn toward. Missing is costlier than clumsy.

6. **Validate without agreeing.** Attend, reflect, name the valid, refuse the invalid. Keep disagreement inside relatedness.

7. **Cheap, declineable diagnostics only.** One small conflicting-interest moment is worth more than ten lunches. State the ask so no is free. Do not stage a loyalty test.

8. **If you have rank: invert the default, as a script.** Ask a question you do not know the answer to before you tell. Invite a specific person by name. Acknowledge one uncertainty. Thank the first correction. Power will not make this feel natural. **[Evidence]** Galinsky et al. (2006); Nembhard and Edmondson (2006); Schein, *Humble Inquiry* (2013).

9. **If you do not have rank: send necessary negative information as task, not as a warmth bid.** Expect MUM and your own inhibition.

10. **Type the violation before repair theater.** Competence miss → apology plus changed behavior. Integrity miss → do not cash niceness. Distrust can stay high while professional calculus-based trust continues.

11. **Maintain without forcing Blue.** For active ties: positivity, shared tasks, occasional assurance of reliability, occasional novel joint problem. Let clean unused ties go dormant.

12. **After a bad turn, read complementarity before color.** Did you send hostile-dominant? Hostility begets hostility. That may be this interaction, not a state change.

13. **Rupture naming when a valued tie strains.** Say the bond/task strained; own your part; renegotiate. Withdrawal needs drawing-out; confrontation needs non-defensive uptake.

14. **Stop condition.** Once you have one diagnostic on integrity, a climate reading on error, and structural temperature on who can harm you — stop treating more familiarity as more of the same. Further contact is maintenance, Discovery, or decay management.

**Relationship-type overlays.** Friendship: 4–6, 11, 13, Michelangelo toward their ideal. Team: 2, 8, voice. Authority above you: 1, 2, 9, 10. Authority in your hands: 8 as the job. Deep Blue: positivity weighting can drop (Tickle-Degnen); anger is allowed; interference is still load-bearing.

### E. Open questions the research does not adequately answer

1. **How to combine the state vector in real time.** The literatures are siloed (couples, teams, therapy). No established method for tracking PPR, climate, ABI, distrust, and secure-base usability as one working model in mixed-role life.

2. **Play as an enabling target outside intimates.** Adult play research and organizational improv are thin, correlational, or commercial. The architecture’s “playful, daring, alive” is richer than team learning. Bateson’s play frame plus secure-base noninterference is a hypothesis, not a literature.

3. **Enabling candor and enabling play at once.** They can trade off (yes-and vs valid no; psychological safety vs play). When, and for whom.

4. **Person-specific trust-model identification without contamination.** Discovery can probe. There is no validated field method that is not just living with someone. Rejection sensitivity and IWMs are measured by questionnaire in labs.

5. **Blueward movement without approval-seeking.** Still open from the alarm note. SDT says: do not trade autonomy for relatedness. Michelangelo says: affirm *their* ideal, not their approval of you. Neither is a tested protocol for a character who has historically converted others’ states into assignments.

6. **Transfer of clinical routines into non-clinical life.** Alliance and MI findings are role-asymmetric. Over-using rupture-naming or reflections at work can itself become a status-odd, evaluation-like act.

7. **How much enabling is possible toward a Red / high-distrust other.** The architecture already shifts to structural influence when reachability is gone. The enabling literatures mostly assume residual goodwill. Deterrence and optionality remain the Red toolkit; they will not produce play.

8. **Cultural and relational-mobility variation.** Most cited samples are WEIRD couples and Western firms. What counts as a bid, as interference, as integrity, and as play shifts. Not mapped here.

9. **Somatic layer.** Social Baseline and Dickerson/Kemeny give bodily mechanisms for safety and evaluative threat. They do not tell this character how an alarm episode ends. That remains the unfinished technique domain from the alarm note.

10. **Whether “best relational state with you” is theirs or yours.** Michelangelo is supposed to be *their* ideal self. The founder list (playful, bold, candid…) is a specific aesthetic. If that is *your* preferred other, it is Pygmalion. The research cannot bless the list without asking whether it is theirs.

## IX. Distilled corrections to the working model

Keep: disposition ≠ emotion; slack; load-bearing conditions; person-specific evidence; social vs structural temperature; the anti-manipulation clause; Discovery as how you learn the transition function.

Subdivide: Blue–Red into a summary plus a state vector. Relative path into exposure / predictability / micro-responsiveness / novelty. Evidence path into diagnostic situations, costly signals, and violation-type.

Replace: “invite expression” as the engine → availability, noninterference, and response to *their* bids. “Relational entropy” → maintenance / decay / dormancy / stall. “Build trust” as the goal → secure-base usability and perceived responsiveness, which sometimes produce trust as a by-product.

Add: epistemic trust; decline-without-cost; interference as a load-bearing inhibitor inside Blue; leader inclusiveness as a different act from peer goodwill; dormant ties as a third fate besides Blueward and Redward.

The principle that survives, restated in the literature’s language:

> Remove non-diagnostic social threat. Support the other’s autonomy. Be available without occupying their action. Respond to the bids they already made. Let disagreement stay inside the bond. Do not manufacture tests they cannot refuse.

That is closer to Feeney’s secure base and Reis’s responsiveness than to charisma, and closer to Edmondson’s climate when you hold rank. It is not a color. It is a set of recurring processes.
